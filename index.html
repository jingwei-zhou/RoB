模型口径：月度摊销引擎，年度汇总。买房侧终值＝房屋市值 − 贷款余额 − 卖出费用 − 超出自住豁免部分的资本利得税 ＋ 买方投资组合（税后）；租房侧终值＝租方投资组合（税后）。租方初始投入＝首付＋买入过户费；此后每月由支出较少的一方把两边现金流差额投入自己的组合，按相同回报率复利。税务按 2026 年联邦规则（OBBBA）：标准扣除单身 $16,100 / 合报 $32,200（随费用通胀调整）；SALT 上限 $40,400，2029 年前每年 +1%，MAGI 超过 $505K 按 30% 递减（最低 $10K），2030 年起回到 $10,000；房贷利息抵扣本金上限 $750K；37% 档的逐项扣除按 35% 计；PMI 视同房贷利息抵扣（AGI $100K–$110K 逐步取消）。自住满 2 年可豁免 $250K / $500K 增值。州税：资本利得按所填州税率征收；可选新泽西式州地税抵扣（租客按房租 18% 折算）。未建模：州层面的房贷利息抵扣、组合每年的股息税拖累、再融资、地税重估上限（如加州 Prop 13）、空置与搬家成本。

Method: monthly amortization engine, summarized by year. Buy-side value = home value − loan balance − selling costs − capital-gains tax above the home-sale exclusion + the buyer's investment portfolio (after tax). Rent-side value = the renter's portfolio (after tax). The renter starts with the down payment + buying closing costs invested; after that, each month whichever side spends less invests the difference at the same return. Taxes follow 2026 federal rules (OBBBA): standard deduction $16,100 single / $32,200 joint (indexed to cost inflation); SALT cap $40,400, +1% a year through 2029, reduced by 30% of MAGI above $505K (floor $10K), reverting to $10,000 in 2030; mortgage-interest deduction on up to $750K of debt; itemized deductions worth 35% in the 37% bracket; PMI treated as deductible interest (phased out at AGI $100K–$110K). Home-sale exclusion $250K / $500K after 2 years. State: capital gains taxed at your state rate; optional New Jersey–style property-tax deduction (renters at 18% of rent). Not modeled: state mortgage-interest deductions, annual dividend tax drag, refinancing, assessment caps (e.g., California Prop 13), vacancy and moving costs.

这是一个用来自己调参数的测算工具，不构成税务、法律或投资建议。做决定前请让会计师核对你的实际税率和地税评估值。默认利率来自 Freddie Mac PMMS，税务参数来自 IRS 2026 年通胀调整。所有计算都在你的浏览器里完成，不上传任何数据。
